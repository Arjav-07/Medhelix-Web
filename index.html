  1. 01

    History

    Start with the story. Ask open questions, follow the thread, and build a timeline that actually fits.

  2. 02

    Examine

    Move from story to signs. Focus your exam on what would change your mind.

  3. 03

    Investigate

    Order what answers the question. Every test costs time and money — just like on the ward.

  4. 04

    Treat

    Commit to a plan, then watch the patient respond. The debrief shows what your decision changed.

A question bank asks you to recall. MedHelix asks you to decide: you take a history, examine, investigate and treat, then see how the patient responds. The reasoning is the point, not the answer key.

Cases are written and reviewed with clinicians and educators. They are fictional composites built for learning — not records of real patients, and not a protocol to follow at work.
