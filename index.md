# MedHelix — Clinical Reasoning & Interactive Medical Cases

> Learn medicine through decisions. MedHelix is a medical education platform where learners practise clinical reasoning through interactive patient cases.

## The Problem with Traditional Medical Studying
Medical school and exam prep often rely on multiple-choice question banks. While MCQs test recall, clinical practice demands decision-making under uncertainty. On the wards, patients do not present with four choices; you must formulate a differential diagnosis, select appropriate investigations, and initiate timely treatment.

## How MedHelix Works
One case. Four critical clinical steps:

1. **History**: Interview the patient using conversational AI dialogue. Uncover onset, triggers, and medical history.
2. **Examine**: Perform physical checks and monitor live vitals (HR, BP, SpO₂, RR).
3. **Investigate**: Order ECGs, lab panels, bedside ultrasound, or blood tests without spoilers.
4. **Treat**: Administer medications, interventions, or referrals and see immediate patient outcome feedback.

## Features
- **AI Patient Chat**: Natural dialogue simulating diverse clinical communication styles.
- **Dynamic Vitals Monitor**: Real-time physiological trends in response to interventions.
- **Five-Attempt Hints**: Scaffolding that guides your diagnostic process without giving away the diagnosis.
- **AI Tutor Debrief**: Evidence-based feedback on diagnostic accuracy, test efficiency, and clinical safety.
- **90-Second Ward Challenges**: Micro-cases designed to fit between ward rounds and clinic sessions.
- **XP & Streaks**: Habit-forming progress tracking for daily clinical improvement.
