Privacy Notice

Last updated: November 2026 · Pre-Release Waitlist Notice

This privacy notice explains how MedHelix ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and safeguards information when you register for early access or join our pre-release waitlist at medhelixapp.com.

1. Information We Collect

When you join the MedHelix waitlist, we collect minimal personal information necessary to deliver early access:

  • Email address: To inform you of your position, notify you when your early access cohort opens, and send pre-release updates.
  • Role / Professional stage: (e.g., Medical Student, Foundation Doctor, Clinician, Educator) to tailor cases to your clinical stage.
  • Referral code & timestamp: To attribute invitations and position queue counts.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information strictly for the following purposes:

  • Assigning your queue position in our founding cohort.
  • Notifying you when pre-release access and beta builds are available.
  • Sending occasional updates regarding key features, verified clinical cases, and product milestones.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to advertisers or third-party brokers.

3. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your email address based on your explicit consent provided when submitting the waitlist form. You may withdraw your consent at any time.

4. Data Storage and Security

Waitlist records are stored securely with encrypted storage and access-controlled databases. We retain your email only until the application launches and your account is onboarded, or until you choose to unsubscribe.

5. Your Rights

Under applicable data privacy regulations (including GDPR and UK GDPR), you have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction or deletion of your waitlist record.
  • Unsubscribe or opt out of pre-release communications at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, simply email us at info@medhelixapp.com with the subject "Privacy Request".
